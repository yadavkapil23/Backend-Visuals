Sessions

The Analogy

The Coat Check. You give your coat to the clerk. They put it in a specific slot (Server Memory) and give you a ticket number #123 (Session ID). To get your coat back, you must go to the same counter, or the clerks must share a central list.

check_circle
Easily Revokable

Server can just delete the session ID to ban a user instantly.

check_circle
Smaller Payload

Cookie just contains a small ID (e.g., "sess_abc123").

cancel
Harder to Scale

Need "Sticky Sessions" or a central Redis database so all servers know the ID.

badge

Tokens (JWT)

The Analogy

The Hotel Key Card. The front desk gives you a card with your room number and expiry date digitally encoded on it. You can swipe it at any door. The door lock doesn't need to call the front desk; it just validates the card's digital signature.

check_circle
Stateless & Scalable

Server B doesn't need to know you logged in on Server A. It just verifies the signature.

check_circle
Cross-Domain

Easier to use across mobile apps and different domains (CORS friendly).

cancel
Hard to Revoke

Once issued, a token is valid until it expires. You can't easily "delete" it server-side.
