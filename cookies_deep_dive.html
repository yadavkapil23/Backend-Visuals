Session Cookies

Created without an expiration date. They exist only in RAM and vanish the moment you close the browser window. Used for temporary states.

save

Persistent Cookies

Have a Max-Age or Expires date. They are saved to the hard drive and survive even after a reboot, until they expire. Used for "Remember Me".

public_off

Third-Party Cookies

Set by a domain other than the one you are visiting (e.g., ads, trackers). Browsers are actively blocking these to protect privacy.
