password

1. Something You Know

The most common factor. Passwords, PINs, or answers to secret questions. Weakness: Can be stolen, guessed, or phished.

smartphone

2. Something You Have

A physical device. Your smartphone, a hardware key (YubiKey), or a smart card. Strength: Even if a hacker has your password, they don't have your phone.

fingerprint

3. Something You Are

Biometrics. Fingerprints, Face ID, Iris scans, or voice patterns. Benefit: Extremely hard to replicate or steal.
