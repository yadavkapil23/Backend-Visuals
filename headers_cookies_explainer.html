Step 1: The Stranger

You visit the website for the first time. The server doesn't know who you are.

mail Analogy: Headers

Headers are like the outside of an envelope.

  • badge
    User-Agent: "I am a Chrome Browser on Windows" (Like a return address).
  • translate
    Accept-Language: "Please reply in English" (Like 'Fragile' or instructions).
  • sd_card
    Content-Type: "This request contains JSON data" (Describes what's inside).

cookie Analogy: Cookies

Cookies are like a VIP Wristband or coat check ticket given to you by the venue.

  • download
    Set-Cookie (Header): The venue gives you the wristband when you enter (Login).
  • upload
    Cookie (Header): You show the wristband every time you order a drink so they know you paid.
  • timer_off
    Expiration: The wristband only works for tonight (Session cookies) or for a year (Persistent cookies).
